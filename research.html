Sustainable supply chain management

Research

I examine the organizational and behavioural conditions that enable substantive responses to supply chain sustainability concerns.

Research projects

Ethical Climate and Supplier Selection: Unit Price versus Sustainability Performance

A behavioural study of how organizational ethical climate and cost–sustainability trade-offs influence purchasing professionals’ supplier recommendations.

Sustainable Public Procurement

Research on how municipal employees interpret sustainability objectives and develop capabilities to implement sustainable purchasing practices.

Psychological Distance, Action Identity, and Sustainability Trade-offs in Supplier Selection

Research on how psychological distance and the meaning assigned to supplier selection shape individuals’ willingness to prioritize sustainability.

Forced Labour Risk Reporting in Canadian Supply Chains

Research on how organizations communicate responses to forced labour risk, with attention to substantive and symbolic disclosure.
