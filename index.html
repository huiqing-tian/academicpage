About me

I am on the 2026–27 academic job market.

I am a fifth-year Ph.D. candidate in Administration in the Operations Management and Information Systems area at the Schulich School of Business, York University, under the supervision of Dr. David Johnston.

My research examines how organizations and individuals navigate the competing yet interdependent economic, social, and environmental objectives of sustainable supply chain management. I study sustainable supplier selection, organizational capabilities for sustainable supply chain management, and supply chain transparency using data collected from behavioural experiments, qualitative interviews, and corporate disclosure reports.

Before joining Schulich, I completed a Master of Environment and Sustainability at Western University and an Honours B.Sc. in Integrated Science (Biology) at McMaster University. My doctoral research is supported by a Canada Graduate Scholarship–Doctoral (SSHRC).
