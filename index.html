About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Administration in the Operations Management and Information Systems area at the Schulich School of Business, York University.

My research examines how organizations and individuals respond to supply chain sustainability concerns. I study how organizational and behavioural conditions shape decisions when economic, environmental, and social objectives compete for attention.

My work uses behavioural experiments, qualitative inquiry, and statistical modelling, with a focus on sustainable supplier selection, sustainable public procurement, and forced labour risk disclosure.

My teaching focuses on helping students become thoughtful and effective decision-makers through analytical tools, case discussions, and experiential learning.
